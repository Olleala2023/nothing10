Email Setup Guide

← Back to App

Last updated: October 28, 2024

Goal: Set up contact@nothing10.com email address to receive user feedback and inquiries.

This guide covers the most common and cost-effective methods for setting up email on your domain.

Why This Matters

Having a professional email address like contact@nothing10.com helps build trust with users and provides a way for them to reach out with questions, feedback, or support requests.

Method 1: Cloudflare Email Routing (Recommended)

Cost: Free | Difficulty: Easy | Setup Time: 5-10 minutes

  1. Log into Cloudflare Dashboard
    Go to your Cloudflare account and select the nothing10.com domain
  2. Enable Email Routing
    Go to Email → Email Routing → Get Started
  3. Add DNS Records
    Cloudflare will automatically add the required MX and TXT records
  4. Create Email Address
    Go to Email → Email Routing → Email Addresses → Create Address
    Enter: contact as the local part
  5. Set Destination
    Enter your personal email address (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) as the destination
  6. Verify Setup
    Send a test email to contact@nothing10.com to verify it works

💡 Pro Tip: You can create multiple email addresses (support@, hello@, etc.) and forward them all to the same personal inbox.

Method 2: Gmail with Custom Domain

Cost: $6/month per user | Difficulty: Medium | Setup Time: 15-30 minutes

  1. Sign up for Google Workspace
    Go to workspace.google.com and start a free trial
  2. Verify Domain Ownership
    Add the required TXT record to your domain's DNS
  3. Set up Email
    Create a user account with the email address contact@nothing10.com
  4. Configure DNS
    Update your domain's MX records to point to Google's servers
  5. Test Email
    Send a test email to verify everything works

Method 3: Zoho Mail (Free Tier)

Cost: Free for up to 5 users | Difficulty: Medium | Setup Time: 10-20 minutes

  1. Sign up for Zoho Mail
    Go to mail.zoho.com and create a free account
  2. Add Your Domain
    Go to Control Panel → Domains → Add Domain
  3. Verify Domain
    Add the required TXT record to verify domain ownership
  4. Configure DNS
    Update MX records to point to Zoho's servers
  5. Create Email Account
    Create contact@nothing10.com user account

Method 4: Simple Email Forwarding

Cost: $1-5/month | Difficulty: Easy | Setup Time: 5-15 minutes

  1. Choose Email Provider
    Popular options: Namecheap, GoDaddy, Porkbun, or your domain registrar
  2. Enable Email Forwarding
    Most registrars offer email forwarding as an add-on service
  3. Set up Forwarding
    Forward contact@nothing10.com to your personal email
  4. Configure DNS
    Add MX records if required by your provider

DNS Records You'll Need

Depending on your chosen method, you may need to add these DNS records:

MX Records (Mail Exchange)

  • Cloudflare: Usually auto-configured
  • Gmail: ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM (priority 1)
  • Zoho: mx.zoho.com (priority 10)

TXT Records (Domain Verification)

  • Google: google-site-verification=...
  • Zoho: zoho-verification=...
  • Cloudflare: Usually auto-configured

Testing Your Setup

Once configured, test your email setup:

  1. Send a test email from your personal email to contact@nothing10.com
  2. Check that it arrives in your destination inbox
  3. Reply to the email to test the reverse direction
  4. Test from different email providers (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)

Best Practices

  • Use a professional email client to manage your business email
  • Set up email filters to organize incoming messages
  • Create an auto-reply for common inquiries
  • Monitor spam folders for legitimate emails
  • Consider multiple addresses (support@, hello@, etc.) for different purposes

⚠️ Important: Keep your email credentials secure and consider using two-factor authentication for your email accounts.
