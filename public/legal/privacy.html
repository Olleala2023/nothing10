Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 17, 2026

TL;DR: We don't collect your personal data. Your app data stays on your device. We use Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics. No accounts. Just you and your pause.

1. Information We Don't Collect

10 Minutes of Nothing is designed to be private. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you, including:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your specific app interactions or timer sessions
  • Your exact location (we only collect general country-level data via Google Analytics)
  • Your timer sessions or statistics (these stay on your device)

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics (see section 3 below), but this data cannot identify you personally.

2. Local Data Storage

All your data stays on your device using your browser's local storage (not cookies):

  • Timer Statistics: Your completed sessions, weekly minutes, and streak days are stored locally
  • App Settings: Your preferred duration and display mode preferences
  • A/B Test Bucket: Which motivational phrase you see (randomly assigned once)
  • Usage Flags: Whether you've used fullscreen mode (to hide the button)
  • Personal Reflections: Your thoughts and notes from completed sessions

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any servers. Local storage is more secure than cookies and doesn't track you across different websites.

3. Web Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how our service is used and to improve user experience. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data, including:

  • Page views and navigation patterns
  • General geographic location (country-level, not city)
  • Device type and browser information
  • Time spent on pages

This data is collected anonymously and cannot be used to identify you personally. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by:

  • Installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Using privacy-focused browsers or browser extensions that block analytics
  • Adjusting your browser's cookie settings

We do not use Facebook Pixel, advertising networks, or other third-party tracking services beyond Google Analytics.

4. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to store app data locally on your device:

  • Timer Statistics: Your completed sessions, weekly minutes, and streak days
  • App Settings: Your preferred duration and display mode preferences
  • A/B Test Bucket: Which motivational phrase you see (randomly assigned once)
  • Usage Flags: Whether you've used fullscreen mode (to hide the button)
  • Personal Reflections: Your thoughts and notes from completed sessions

This app data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any servers. Local storage is different from cookies - it's more secure and doesn't track you across websites. Note: Google Analytics uses cookies for analytics purposes (see section 3).

5. Contact Information

If you choose to contact us via email, we will only use your email address to respond to your inquiry. We will not:

  • Add you to any mailing lists
  • Share your email with third parties
  • Use your email for marketing purposes
  • Store your email beyond our conversation

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed to be safe for users of all ages. Since we don't collect any personal information, there are no special considerations for children's privacy. Parents can use the Focus Lock guide to help children stay focused during their sessions.

7. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, your data security depends on your device's security measures. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Using a secure browser
  • Not sharing your device with others if you want to keep your statistics private

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Since we don't collect your contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of Google Analytics: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or use privacy-focused browser extensions
  • Clear your app data: Your statistics and settings are stored locally on your device using local storage and can be cleared by:
    • Clearing your browser's local storage for this site
    • Uninstalling and reinstalling the app
    • Using your browser's developer tools to clear site data
    • Using your browser's privacy/incognito mode
  • Request information: Contact us at contact@nothing10.com if you have questions about your data
