About "10 Minutes of Nothing"

We make one simple ritual: open the app, start the timer, and do nothing for 10 minutes. No accounts, no tracking, all data stays local in your browser.

Why? In a world of constant distractions, pausing is a skill. Our PWA works offline, supports Hidden, Ghost, and Breathing modes, and when finished, invites you to write one thought and do one small action.

"Sometimes the best thing you can do is nothing."
